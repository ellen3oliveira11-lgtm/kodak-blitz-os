Blitz OS Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Blitz OS is a desktop video-editing application. This policy describes information handled by the application and its optional online features.

Information stored on your device

Video and image editing is generally performed on your device. The application may store your settings, job history, downloaded assets, and API credentials locally so its features can work. Protect your device and any local files containing credentials.

Optional third-party services

If you use an online or AI feature, the information needed for that feature may be sent to its service provider. Depending on the feature, this can include search terms, prompts, selected images or video, extracted audio, text for speech generation, or media and metadata you choose to publish. The application does not operate those providers; their own privacy policies and terms govern their handling of information they receive.

Integrated features may connect to services such as Google Gemini, Groq, ElevenLabs, Pinterest, YouTube, Spotify, Jamendo, and the configured publishing provider. These connections are used only when the relevant feature is invoked or configured.

Pinterest

The Pinterest integration is used to search for public Pins using the search terms you submit, display available image previews, and download selected images into the local sticker library. It uses configured Pinterest API credentials for this access. The application does not use this integration to access your private Pinterest boards, followers, or account content.

Retention and your choices

Local settings, history, and downloaded files remain on your device until you remove them. Information sent to a third-party service may be retained by that provider under its policies. You can avoid optional online features by not using them and can remove locally stored files or credentials from your device.

Children

Blitz OS is a general-purpose desktop application and is not directed to children under 13. Do not provide children's personal information to online features.
